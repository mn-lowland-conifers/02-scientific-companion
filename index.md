# Science Companion to the Legislative Report

**Lowland Conifer Peatland Carbon in Minnesota: Methods, Mapping, Field Validation, and a
Systematic Review of Harvesting & Disturbance Impacts**

**Status:** Structure only — not written yet.

## Relationship to the legislative report
Legislative report = distilled, policy-facing, options-not-advocacy.
This companion = complete, reproducible, neutral; management implications are stated but no policy.
Shared figures are generated once here and reused there.

## Relationship to 03-peatland-carbon-modeling
Part I of this document (sections 2-6: data/covariates, peatland probability, peat depth,
decomposition state, carbon stock) covers the same modeling work documented in full in
`03-peatland-carbon-modeling` (chapters x02-x04). For now, Part I chapters here will
**reference/link out to 03 rather than duplicate it**. Revisit this if Part I needs to stand
alone as a publishable manuscript (see modular design below).

## Modular design
Four parts. Parts I-II (statewide DSM + field validation) form one publishable manuscript;
Part III (systematic review/meta-analysis) is a second standalone review paper; Part IV
integrates the two. Spin-off targets are noted at each part head.
