## 9.1 Coring & Depth

Peat cores were collected in the field with a Russian peat auger. At each sampling point the auger was inserted vertically into the ground with minimal disturbance to the sampling area, and then rotated 180° to collect a sample. Coring proceeded in until mineral contact was reached and the total depth to mineral depth was recorded at that point. Independent of the core, peat depth was also probed directly at the vegetation plots using a compass. Three points were located 5m from the vegetation plot center at 60°, 180°, and 300°. A tile probe was then inserted at each until resistance of further probing indicated mineral contact. Additional peat depth values were recorded at each degree point, along with any probing difficulties or notes. 

Each extracted core was placed on a plastic gutter sequenually to ‘rebuild’ the soil profile as it existed at the point. Cores were then horizonated and described recording characteristics such as Von Post humification score (H1-H10), fiber content (%), and Munsell color.

## 9.2 Bulk Density

Bulk density samples were taken immediately upon core extraction from half of the pulled auger sample. Volume of the auger was known and the length of each sample was measured to be used in bulk density equation, typically a length of 5cm. Samples were cut with a putty knife directly from the intact core and then bagged in labeled bags with sample ID, depth, and date. Field masses of the samples were recorded at the end of each sampling week.

Bulk density is calculated from the known auger volume and the sample's oven-dry weight after lab processing. ‘Db (g/cm³) = oven-dry weight (g) / volume (cm³).

## 9.3 Laboratory

Sample prep and analysis followed the NRCS Soil Survey Field and Laboratory Methods Manual (SSIR 51). Each sample was split for different procedures depending on amount of sample need based on priority methods

%C (carbon): sent to an AgVise for total C/N by dry combustion 
Rubber Fiber was assessed twice both in the field in an lab:
- Field: von Post humification score during core description
- Lab: fiber content (%) via the rubbed fiber method, converted to fibric/hemic/sapric
